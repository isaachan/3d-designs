## 正面略俯视｜03-front-view.png

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: additional camera-angle rendering of the SAME miniature parking garage, one single landscape product image.
Input image: the supplied image is the approved Concept 01, the authoritative object-identity and geometry reference.
Primary request: show exactly the same physical object from a new camera position. Treat this as a virtual camera orbit around one fixed product. Preserve the design; change only the viewpoint and the lighting naturally needed to reveal it. Do not repeat the supplied camera angle.
Object invariants: one rectangular open-front four-level parking garage beside one circular open-center spiral road tower on a common black base. The garage is on the LEFT and spiral on the RIGHT when viewed from the FRONT; this fixed physical arrangement must be transformed correctly by the new camera, not mirrored or rearranged. Exactly four horizontal parking surfaces including ground and open-air top, three gaps between them, no extra roof or fifth floor. Exactly three complete rising revolutions of ONE continuous helical road connect 1F to 4F. Each level has an open flat connector from the front aisle to a corresponding spiral landing; pillars and guardrails do not block these junctions. Keep the relative dimensions, annular road width, radius, pitch, rounded black posts, thin parking plates, black rear parking wall and low ramp curbs seen in the reference. The spiral remains a sloped road, not stacked flat circular balconies or a staircase.
Appearance invariants: near-black plastic structure and dark asphalt-like road, white parking lines, white lane dashes and arrows, blue accessible bays, light-blue hatched areas and zebra crossings. Suspended parking slab edges from bottom upward: light blue, dark blue, red. Spiral curb accents follow the same source design. Bay counts bottom to top 2,2,3,3. Preserve the miniature cars as consistent scale cues, with wheels resting on their actual surfaces. Preserve both signs in their fixed positions and orientations: black billboard above the rear parking wall, blue left support and red right support, front lettering exactly "Lovely Cars" / "I've Driven"; dark-blue vertical slogan plaque on the outer ramp support, front lettering exactly "HORSEPOWER LEFT," / "BRAINPOWER RIGHT". Where a sign faces away from the camera, show its plain black back or thin edge, never invent duplicate front lettering on its reverse or rotate it to face the camera.
Presentation: polished photoreal industrial product concept render matching the reference, warm off-white seamless studio background, soft diffuse shadows, all black forms readable, landscape framing, entire object including base and signs in frame with generous margins. One coherent full-object view. No montage, inset, split panel, diagram, dimension lines, title, caption, people, books or unrelated objects. Maintain the same black/light-blue/dark-blue/red palette.
Camera for this image: almost straight-on FRONT view, centered in front of the complete assembly, only 5 degrees of horizontal orbit from a true front elevation, camera elevation 12 degrees looking slightly down. Use long-lens/near-orthographic product perspective with limited foreshortening. Show all four garage fronts aligned vertically on the image left and the spiral silhouette on image right. The lower camera angle should clearly reveal the level heights, unobstructed connector openings and rising road profile; keep the fourth-floor top and billboard visible. This must look much straighter and lower than the reference camera, not another front three-quarter image.
