## 01｜四层停车场主概念图

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: industrial design concept rendering of a desktop miniature parking garage, landscape canvas.
Primary request: Develop only the garage portion of the supplied garage-bookshelf into a standalone FOUR-LEVEL miniature parking garage with a continuous external helical driving road that a miniature car can follow from level 1 all the way to level 4. Replace the bookshelf area with the helical road. This is a concept, not a finished CAD product.
Input images: Image 1 is the latest garage-bookshelf v5 reference for black material, cylindrical columns, colored slab edges, top billboard, and side slogan plaque. Image 2 is the parking floor marking reference; use its markings and 2/2/3/3 bay arrangement but use the black palette of Image 1, not its gray/yellow palette.
Subject and geometry: On one common black base, place the open-front rectangular four-level parking stack beside a large open-center helical road tower. Exactly FOUR horizontal parking surfaces, including the ground floor and open-air fourth floor, with THREE gaps between them. No extra roof slab and no fifth floor. Preserve the garage's compact rectangular plates, round black posts, rear panel, and parking bay proportions. Expand the front maneuvering aisle as necessary to support a usable connection. The separate spiral is the main new feature: ONE continuous solid annular road ribbon with THREE complete rising revolutions, rising one level per revolution. It starts flush with floor 1 and ends flush with floor 4. At each floor height provide a short horizontal landing and a broad unobstructed connector into that floor's front driving aisle. Clearly show the connections at floors 2 and 3. The driveable ribbon must remain continuous across the floor connections and never be interrupted by columns, guardrails, wall panels, gaps, steps, or floating ends. Low inner and outer curbs follow both sides of the road, with deliberate openings at the landing junctions. A few thin support posts sit OUTSIDE the clear roadway. Show sufficient vertical separation between successive revolutions for cars to pass. The helix road is a wide driving surface, not stairs, stacked flat circular balconies, or a decorative coil.
Relative proportions: miniature cars approximately 85 x 38 mm; original parking stack width about 200 mm and floor pitch about 63 mm; concept driveway clear width about 70 mm and helix centerline radius about 120 mm. The helix outside diameter is about 310 mm, visibly wider than the rectangular parking stack. These are proportion guides, do not add dimension lines to the image.
Materials and colors: matte near-black #16181B structural parts, black asphalt-like decks, white crisp parking stripes/numbers/arrows/zebra crossing, light-blue hatched exclusion areas, dark-blue accessible parking accents on the two lower floors. Preserve the three suspended slab edges from bottom to top: light blue #39A9DB, dark blue #151B65, red #ED242A. Helix curbs use restrained light-blue/dark-blue/red segment accents corresponding to floor heights, road remains black. Use believable sturdy 3D-printable plastic construction rather than full-size concrete architecture.
Preserved signage: Keep the black rectangular billboard above the rear of the fourth parking level, white raised lettering on two lines, exactly "Lovely Cars" and "I've Driven"; left post light blue and right post red. Keep the dark-blue bolt-on side slogan plaque, white text exactly "HORSEPOWER LEFT," and "BRAINPOWER RIGHT", placed on an exterior rear support where it cannot obstruct the roadway. Do not add logos to either sign.
Vehicles: show a small number of realistic miniature cars as scale cues, at least one on the lower helix and one on an upper helix section, all wheels resting on the road and body following its slope. Leave ample unoccupied road so its continuous route is legible. Parked cars sit fully within marked bays. No people, books, trees, shops, elevators, or unrelated props.
Composition and style: polished physical product concept render, front three-quarter elevated view that exposes the garage's open front, the complete helical tower, and the intermediate landing connections. Wide landscape framing, entire object uncropped, warm off-white seamless studio background, soft diffuse shadows, crisp edges and readable black forms. The spiral should be visually dominant but not hide the parking levels. Beautiful restrained industrial design presentation, photoreal materials with a clear concept-model quality. Add only discreet floor labels "1F", "2F", "3F", "4F" near the connector landings; no title, paragraphs, legends, or dimension annotations.
Constraints: exactly four floors, exactly three ascending turns, the same single road connects all four levels, keep both original signs and recognizable M-color detailing, remove all bookshelf geometry. Avoid impossible intersections, disappearing road, blocked exits, steep short ramps masquerading as a helix, yellow rails, oversized cars, extra floors, duplicated signs, gibberish text, watermark.
